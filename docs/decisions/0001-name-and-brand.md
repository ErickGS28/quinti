# 0001. Name and brand: Quinti

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The project needs a memorable name with a Latin American identity that evokes its purpose.

## Decision
**Quinti**, from *qínti* or *kinti*, which means hummingbird in Quechua. The logo will be a hummingbird (pending delivery).

## Why
The hummingbird is small, fast and goes straight where it is needed: it is the image of help that is small, fast and direct. The name is short, pronounceable in Spanish and English, and has roots in the Andean region.

## Consequences
- Brand tone: warm, direct, hopeful and without drama.
- The interface accent color is defined with the logo.
- Check name availability (domain, repository, username) before publishing.
